3. VARI (Visible Atmospherically Resistant Index): (G-R)/(G+R-B)
4. NGRDI (Normalized Green-Red Difference Index): (G-R)/(G+R)
5. NGBDI (Normalized Green-Blue Difference Index): (G-B)/(G+B)
6. ExG (Excess Green): 2*g-r-b (where r,g,b are normalized RGB)
7. ExR (Excess Red): 1.4*r-g (where r,g,b are normalized RGB)
8. ExB (Excess Blue): 1.4*b-g (where r,g,b are normalized RGB)
9. ExGR (Excess Green minus Excess Red): ExG - ExR
10. CIVE (Color Index of Vegetation Extraction): 
0.441*r-0.811*g+0.385*b+18.78745
11. VEG (Vegetativeness): g/(r^a * b^(1-a)) where a=0.667
12. COM (Combination): 0.25*ExG + 0.30*ExGR + 0.33*CIVE + 0.12*VEG
13. TGI (Triangular Greenness Index): g-0.39*r-0.61*b
14. MGRVI (Modified Green Red Vegetation Index): (G²-R²)/(G²+R²)
15. RGVBI (Red Green Blue Vegetation Index): (G-(B*R))/(G²+(B*R))
